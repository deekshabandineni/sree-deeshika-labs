Our Testing Services

Comprehensive pharmaceutical testing and quality assurance solutions

📊 API Testing

Complete analysis of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients including purity, potency, and contaminant detection using advanced chromatography techniques.

💊 Formulation Analysis

End-to-end testing of finished pharmaceutical products for dissolution, content uniformity, and stability profile assessment.

🧪 Impurity Profiling

Detailed characterization of organic and inorganic impurities using HPLC, GC, and ICP-OES methods.

📋 Method Development

Custom analytical method development and validation services tailored to your specific product requirements.

⏱️ Stability Testing

Long-term and accelerated stability studies under controlled conditions per ICH guidelines.

🔍 Microbial Testing

Comprehensive microbiological analysis including microbial limit tests, sterility testing, and endotoxin detection.
